# ImportedInflation
"What impact does a shock in energy prices have on bilateral trade between France and Germany, and to what extent does Germany (which is more exposed) pass on persistent imported inflation to France via its exports?"

Modeling of imported inflation in MatLab and Dynare. 

## Model 
External constraint : effect of energy price shock on the exchanges between two countries with different energy mixes. 
Initial model : @soe_standard.mod

## Chosen Data
Comparison of France and Germany. 
Developed economies with different energy mixes. 
Use of DBnomics throug @call_dbnomics.m

## References
"Monetary Policy and Exchange Rate Volatility in a Small Open Economy", Jordi Gal (CREI, UPF, CEPR and NBER) and Tommaso Monacelli (IGIER, Universita Bocconi and CEPR)
